Count the words in this text, ignoring case and the punctuation around words:

"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog sleeps; the fox runs!"

Print the three most frequent words with their counts (most frequent first; words with the
same count in alphabetical order), then the number of distinct words, the longest word (the
alphabetically first one if several are equally long), and, in the order they first appear,
the distinct words that contain the letter "o".

The program must print exactly:

```
the: 4
dog: 2
fox: 2
distinct: 10
longest: sleeps
words with o: brown, fox, over, dog
```
